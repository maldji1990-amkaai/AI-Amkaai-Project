AMKAAI FINAL PATCH — VIDEO INFRASTRUCTURE + CREATIVE STUDIO

This patch supersedes all previous AMKAAI patches. Use this ZIP only.

MAIN VIDEO STACK
- Wan2.2-TI2V-5B as the default video model.
- RTX 4090 Pod rented on demand through the RunPod REST API.
- Redis + BullMQ queue.
- 5-second video clips.
- 5 Credits per second for the user.
- 30 seconds = 6 clips = 150 Credits.
- GPU is reused while there is active/queued work.
- GPU is terminated after the configured idle grace period.
- Failed/cancelled scenes are retried/cancelled individually; completed scenes are not regenerated.
- Actual GPU time is intentionally tracked/observable before changing pricing.

CREATIVE STUDIO
- AI Director / continuity bible.
- Storyboard and scenes.
- Character and voice selection per scene.
- Scene cost preview.
- Generation pipeline and final composition hooks.
- Project/assets/history structure.

PAYMENTS
- PayPal remains the payment gateway.
- No Stripe migration is included.

RUNPOD DIRECT POD REQUIREMENT
The patch manages Pod lifecycle, but the Pod itself must contain a tested Wan 2.2 TI2V-5B HTTP server.
Configure either RUNPOD_POD_TEMPLATE_ID or RUNPOD_POD_IMAGE.
The service contract is documented in RUNPOD-4090-AUTO-MANAGER.md.

REQUIRED ENVIRONMENT
RUNPOD_DIRECT_POD_ENABLED=true
RUNPOD_API_KEY=...
RUNPOD_WEBHOOK_SECRET=...
NEXT_PUBLIC_APP_URL=https://amkaai.net
RUNPOD_POD_TEMPLATE_ID=...
RUNPOD_GPU_TYPE=NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4090
RUNPOD_CLOUD_TYPE=COMMUNITY
RUNPOD_POD_HTTP_PORT=8000
RUNPOD_POD_GENERATE_PATH=/generate
RUNPOD_POD_HEALTH_PATH=/health
RUNPOD_POD_CANCEL_PATH=/cancel
RUNPOD_GPU_IDLE_GRACE_MS=300000
RUNPOD_POD_READY_TIMEOUT_MS=1200000
RUNPOD_POD_REQUEST_TIMEOUT_MS=30000
DEFAULT_VIDEO_MODEL=Wan2.2-TI2V-5B
